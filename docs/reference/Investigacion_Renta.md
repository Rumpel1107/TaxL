# Colombian Individual Income Tax — Verified Normative Reference (Formulario 210, Cédula General, Asalariado): Años Gravables 2023, 2024, 2025

## TL;DR
- The **structural rules of the cédula general are identical across AG 2023, 2024 and 2025** — all governed by Ley 2277 de 2022 (effective AG 2023); no enacted reform changed them, because both the 2024 and the 2025/2026 "leyes de financiamiento" were defeated in Congress (the 2024 project was archived 11-dic-2024; the later reform was sunk 9-dic-2025 in the Comisión Cuarta del Senado by 9 votes to 4). Only the UVT value and its peso conversions change each year (UVT 2023 = $42.412; 2024 = $47.065; 2025 = $49.799).
- Every core parameter is confirmed against primary sources: 25% renta exenta laboral capped at 790 UVT (art. 206-10 E.T.), the 40%/1.340 UVT global cap (art. 336-3), dependents deductions (art. 387 = 10%/384 UVT and art. 336-3 = 72 UVT × up to 4), the art. 241 progressive table (0%–39%), and the filing thresholds (1.400 and 4.500 UVT).
- The 72-UVT-per-dependent deduction and the 1%-electronic-invoice deduction (240 UVT) sit **OUTSIDE** the 40% cap; the 25% exemption, prepaid health, mortgage interest, AFC/voluntary pension and the art. 387 dependents deduction sit **INSIDE** it.

Deadline/threshold decrees: AG 2022 = Decreto 2487 de 16-dic-2022; AG 2023 and following = Decreto 2229 de 22-dic-2023 (permanent calendar). (The AG 2025 gross-income threshold $69.719.000 = 1.400 × $49.799 rounded per art. 868; some publishers show $69.718.600 unrounded.) For asalariados, the additional condition applies that ≥ 80% of income must originate in a labor/legal-and-regulatory relationship.

### 1.3 25% exempt labor income (renta exenta laboral)
- Art. 206 num. 10 E.T., as modified by art. 2 Ley 2277 de 2022: *"El veinticinco por ciento (25%) del valor total de los pagos laborales, limitada anualmente a setecientos noventa (790) UVT."*
- Annual cap 790 UVT: AG 2023 = $33.505.000; AG 2024 = $37.181.000; AG 2025 = $39.341.000.
- Computed after subtracting INCRNGO, deductions and other exemptions from total labor payments. INSIDE the 40% cap.

### 1.4 Dependents deduction — BOTH modalities (post-Ley 2277)
- **(a) Art. 387 E.T.** (inciso 2): up to 10% of labor income by dependents, max 32 UVT monthly = 384 UVT annual. Annual cap: AG 2023 = $16.286.000; AG 2024 = $18.073.000; AG 2025 = $19.123.000. This deduction does NOT increase with the number of dependents. INSIDE the 40% cap.
- **(b) Art. 336 num. 3 E.T.** (inciso 2, added by art. 7 Ley 2277 de 2022): 72 UVT per dependent, up to 4 dependents (max 288 UVT). Per-dependent value: AG 2023 = $3.054.000; AG 2024 = $3.389.000; AG 2025 = $3.586.000. OUTSIDE the 40%/1.340 UVT cap (confirmed by Decreto 2231 de 2023 and DIAN doctrine).
- **Combinability:** A single dependent may generate only ONE of the two deductions, UNLESS the taxpayer has income from a labor relationship (asalariado), in which case BOTH may be applied concurrently on the same dependent (statutory phrase "sin perjuicio de lo establecido en el inciso 2 del artículo 387"). Reglamented by Decreto 2231 de 2023. For a pure asalariado profile, both apply.

### 1.5 Individual caps
| Concept | Cap (UVT) | Article | Inside/outside 40% cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prepaid health (medicina prepagada) | 16 UVT mensual / 192 UVT anual | art. 387 | Inside |
| Mortgage interest (intereses vivienda) | 100 UVT mensual / 1.200 UVT anual | art. 119 | Inside |
| AFC + voluntary pension | 30% of income, max 3.800 UVT | arts. 126-1 y 126-4 | Inside |
| GMF / 4x1000 | 50% of GMF paid | art. 115 | Inside |

Annual peso values — 192 UVT: 2023 $8.143.000 / 2024 $9.036.000 / 2025 $9.561.000. 1.200 UVT: 2023 $50.894.000 / 2024 $56.478.000 / 2025 $59.759.000. 3.800 UVT: 2023 $161.166.000 / 2024 $178.847.000 / 2025 $189.236.000. The 1.200 UVT mortgage-interest cap (art. 119) is confirmed by DIAN Concepto 3591 de 2025 and by arts. 1.2.1.20.3–1.2.1.20.4 DUT 1625 de 2016.

### 1.6 1% electronic-invoice purchases deduction
- Art. 336 num. 5 E.T. (added by art. 7 Ley 2277 de 2022): 1% of purchases of goods/services supported by electronic invoice, max 240 UVT/year.
- 240 UVT: AG 2023 = $10.179.000; AG 2024 = $11.296.000; AG 2025 = $11.952.000.
- Explicitly OUTSIDE the 40% cap: the statute states *"La deducción de que trata el presente numeral no se encuentra sujeta al límite previsto en el numeral 3 del presente artículo... ni podrá dar lugar a pérdidas."* Requires payment via electronic means through a Superfinanciera-supervised entity (num. 5.2/5.3). Reported in casilla 28 of Formulario 210.

### 1.7 General 40% cap on the cédula general
- Art. 336 num. 3 E.T. (art. 7 Ley 2277 de 2022): *"todas las rentas exentas y las deducciones especiales imputables a esta cédula, siempre que no excedan el cuarenta (40%) del resultado del numeral anterior, que en todo caso no puede exceder de mil trescientas cuarenta (1.340) UVT anuales."*
- 1.340 UVT ceiling: AG 2023 = $56.832.000; AG 2024 = $63.067.000; AG 2025 = $66.731.000.
- Base of the 40% = total gross income of the cédula general minus INCRNGO (Decreto reglamentario and art. 1.2.1.20.4 DUT 1625 de 2016; instructivo modified by Resolución 000120 de 2024 to include rentas pasivas de capital and no laborales in the base).
- **INSIDE the cap:** 25% renta exenta laboral, prepaid health, mortgage interest, AFC/voluntary pension, GMF, art. 387 dependents deduction, cesantías, and other exemptions/deductions of the cédula.
- **OUTSIDE the cap:** (i) 72 UVT per dependent (art. 336-3 inciso 2); (ii) 1% electronic-invoice deduction (art. 336-5); (iii) INCRNGO (mandatory pension/health) — subtracted before computing the 40% base; (iv) pension exempt income of the pensions cédula.

### 1.8 INCRNGO — mandatory contributions
- Mandatory pension: art. 55 E.T. (as modified by art. 23 Ley 1943 de 2018) — INCRNGO, no individual limit. Voluntary RAIS contributions also INCRNGO but capped 25%/2.500 UVT.
- Mandatory health: art. 56 E.T. — INCRNGO, no individual limit.
- Fondo de Solidaridad Pensional (FSP): treated as part of the mandatory pension contribution under art. 55 E.T., therefore INCRNGO (mandatory for workers earning ≥ 4 SMMLV, on the same base as pension/health). **Flag:** no specific numbered DIAN Concepto was located confirming FSP INCRNGO status; the treatment rests on FSP being legally part of the mandatory pension contribution (art. 55), as consistently affirmed by Gerencie.com and Actualícese — doctrine rather than a numbered ruling.

### 1.9 Art. 241 E.T. progressive tax table (identical for 2023, 2024, 2025)
Art. 241 E.T. (as modified by art. 34 Ley 2010 de 2019); NOT modified by Ley 2277 de 2022, so identical across all three years. Applied to the consolidated renta líquida gravable per art. 331 E.T.

| Rango UVT (desde–hasta) | Tarifa marginal | Impuesto |
|---|---|---|
| 0 – 1.090 | 0% | 0 |
| >1.090 – 1.700 | 19% | (Base UVT − 1.090) × 19% |
| >1.700 – 4.100 | 28% | (Base − 1.700) × 28% + 116 UVT |
| >4.100 – 8.670 | 33% | (Base − 4.100) × 33% + 788 UVT |
| >8.670 – 18.970 | 35% | (Base − 8.670) × 35% + 2.296 UVT |
| >18.970 – 31.000 | 37% | (Base − 18.970) × 37% + 5.901 UVT |
| >31.000 – en adelante | 39% | (Base − 31.000) × 39% + 10.352 UVT |

The impuesto is expressed in UVT, then converted to pesos with the UVT of the año gravable. The final peso result is rounded to the nearest thousand (art. 577 E.T.).

### 1.10 Reajuste fiscal (art. 70 E.T.)
| Año gravable | Reajuste fiscal | Decree |
|---|---|---|
| 2023 | 12,40% | Decreto 128 de 7-feb-2024 (sustituye art. 1.2.1.17.20 DUT 1625/2016) |
| 2024 | 10,97% | Decreto 174 de 13-feb-2025 |
| 2025 | 5,81% | Decreto 0449 de 2026 |

Decreto 174 de 2025 states verbatim: *"Los contribuyentes podrán ajustar el costo de los activos fijos por el año gravable 2024, en el diez coma noventa y siete por ciento (10,97%)."* The AG 2025 figure (5,81%, matching the IPC clase media used for UVT 2025) was set by Decreto 0449 de 2026 — this is the value that affects the AG 2025 return filed in 2026. Note the reajuste fiscal is relevant to activos fijos / ganancias ocasionales, not to the ordinary labor-income depuration of an asalariado.

### 1.11 Withholding reconciliation and anticipo
- Retención en la fuente practiced during the year (art. 383 E.T., tabla mensual) is credited against the tax in Formulario 210; the taxpayer reconciles retenciones vs. impuesto to determine saldo a pagar or saldo a favor.
- Anticipo (art. 807 E.T.): advance for the following year computed on the current year's net tax minus withholdings, at 25% (first year declaring), 50% (second year), 75% (third and subsequent). Payment in a single cuota for non-grandes-contribuyentes.

## 3. NORMATIVE CHANGES BETWEEN YEARS
- **Structural rules are IDENTICAL across AG 2023, 2024, 2025.** All flow from Ley 2277 de 2022 (effective AG 2023), reglamented by Decreto 2231 de 2023.
- **Corte Constitucional Sentencia C-540 de 12-dic-2023** struck down the 12.000-UVT ceiling for professional-service providers in the SIMPLE regime — this affects the SIMPLE regime, NOT the cédula general / Formulario 210, and does not alter the 40% cap, the 25% exemption or dependents. (The "5.040 UVT" figure occasionally seen relates to a transitional mortgage-interest / rentas-exentas limit issue, not to the ordinary asalariado depuration.)
- **The 2024 "Ley de Financiamiento" (radicada 10-sep-2024) was archived on 11-dic-2024** when the Congress's comisiones económicas adopted the ponencia negativa; it sought roughly $9,8 billones for the 2025 budget and proposed eliminating the 72-UVT dependents deduction and raising top marginal rates. **The later reform (2026 budget) was sunk on 9-dic-2025 in the Comisión Cuarta del Senado by 9 votes against to 4 in favor**, and had sought approximately $16 billones. Senator Efraín Cepeda characterized the defeat as "hoy hundimos la reforma tributaria." Neither project became law.
- Therefore the only inter-year differences are the UVT value and the resulting peso conversions of all UVT-denominated limits.

## 4. FORM 210 LINE MAPPING (AG 2023–2025)
Formulario 210 prescribed by **DIAN Resolución 000044 de 14-mar-2024** *"para el año gravable 2023 y siguientes"* (143 casillas — up from 138 in the AG 2022 form), instructivo modified by **Resolución 000120 de 31-jul-2024**. The SAME form version applies to AG 2023, 2024 and 2025 — line numbering did NOT change between these years (confirm the AG 2025 instructivo edition, "Cartilla… Sistema de Rentas Cedulares 2025," before the 2026 filing season).

Key casillas:
- **Patrimonio:** 29 (patrimonio bruto), 30 (deudas), 31 (patrimonio líquido).
- **Cédula general – rentas de trabajo (laboral):** 32 ingresos brutos, 33 INCRNGO, 34 renta líquida, 36/37 rentas exentas, 39/40 deducciones imputables, 41 rentas exentas y deducciones limitadas, 42 renta líquida ordinaria (R34 = R32 − R33).
- **Rentas de trabajo (honorarios/no laboral-relación):** 43–57. **Rentas de capital:** 58–73. **Rentas no laborales:** 74–90.
- **Consolidation:** 91 renta líquida cédula general (renta líquida bruta acumulada, algebraically returns the pre-benefit figures), 92 (includes the 72-UVT dependents addition + 1% electronic-invoice deduction — the "outside-cap" items), 93 rentas exentas/deducciones limitadas de la cédula general.
- **Special control casillas from Ley 2277:** 28 (1% compras con factura electrónica), 138 (número de dependientes), 139 (adición por dependientes a casilla 92), 140 (superó tope indicativo art. 336-1), 141 (aporte voluntario art. 244-1), 111 (renta líquida gravable art. 241), 118 (dividendos 2ª subcédula art. 240).
- **Liquidación privada:** impuesto sobre la renta, ganancias ocasionales, descuentos, anticipo, retenciones, saldo a pagar / saldo a favor.

## 5. WORKED EXAMPLE (asalariado, AG 2024, UVT = $47.065)
| Step | Concept | Value (COP) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ingresos brutos laborales (casilla 32) | $120.000.000 |
| 2 | (−) INCRNGO: aportes obligatorios salud (art. 56) + pensión + FSP (art. 55) (casilla 33) | ($9.600.000) |
| 3 | = Renta líquida (casilla 34) | $110.400.000 |
| 4 | Renta exenta 25% art. 206-10 (25% × $110,4M = $27,6M; under 790 UVT cap $37.181.000) | $27.600.000 |
| 5 | Deducción dependientes art. 387 (10% of ingresos; under 384 UVT cap $18.073.000) | $11.040.000 |
| 6 | Deducción intereses de vivienda art. 119 (under 1.200 UVT cap) | $10.000.000 |
| 7 | Sum of exemptions + deductions subject to cap = $48.640.000 | — |
| 8 | 40% cap: 40% × $110,4M = $44.160.000; 1.340 UVT ceiling = $63.067.000 → apply the LOWER | −$44.160.000 |
| 9 | = Renta líquida gravable | $66.240.000 |
| 10 | Conversion to UVT: $66.240.000 / $47.065 | 1.407,6 UVT |
| 11 | Art. 241: bracket >1.090–1.700, 19% → (1.407,6 − 1.090) × 19% | 60,35 UVT |
| 12 | Impuesto = 60,35 × $47.065 | ≈ $2.840.000 |

If the 72-UVT-per-dependent (art. 336-3) and 1%-electronic-invoice (art. 336-5) deductions applied, they would be subtracted in casilla 92 AFTER step 8 (they are outside the 40% cap), further reducing the renta líquida gravable.

**Note:** the peso inputs (salary, INCRNGO, dependents, mortgage) are an internally consistent illustration; the rules, caps, UVT values and art. 241 formulas are all primary-source confirmed. A separately published Gerencie.com example (ingresos $300M, INCRNGO $80M → net base $220M; 40% = $88M vs. 1.340-UVT ceiling for AG 2023 = $56.832.000) confirms the 1.340-UVT ceiling prevailing over the 40% when it is the lower figure.

## Recommendations
1. **Hard-code the three UVT values** ($42.412 / $47.065 / $49.799) and drive every limit as `UVT × factor`. Build the art. 241 table as a single shared tax function (identical for all three years).
2. **Implement the 40% cap** as `MIN(0.40 × (ingresos_cédula − INCRNGO), 1.340 × UVT)`, and keep the 72-UVT-per-dependent and 1%-electronic-invoice deductions in a separate "outside-cap" bucket applied in casilla 92 after the capped bucket.
3. **Round per art. 868/577 E.T.:** UVT conversions and final tax to the nearest thousand pesos; keep intermediate UVT values to at least one decimal to avoid drift at bracket boundaries.
4. **Validate** against the worked example above and against the Actualícese/Gerencie Formulario 210 AG 2024 liquidators (casillas 28, 92, 111, 138–141).
5. **Benchmarks that would change the engine:** (a) a new UVT resolution each December (update the year's factor); (b) enactment of any future reforma tributaria altering art. 336 (the 40% cap, the 72-UVT dependents deduction, or the electronic-invoice %) or art. 206-10 (the 790 UVT cap) — none is in force as of the report date, but the government has signaled it will keep trying; (c) a new Formulario 210 prescribing resolution changing casilla numbering.

## Caveats
- **AG 2023 1.340-UVT ceiling = $56.832.000** (1.340 × $42.412). One secondary source (Siempre al Día) printed $53.832.000, which appears to be a typo — the $56.832.000 figure is arithmetically correct and corroborated by the Gerencie worked example. Flagged.
- **FSP INCRNGO treatment** rests on doctrine (art. 55 E.T. as consistently interpreted by Gerencie.com and Actualícese), not on a numbered DIAN Concepto that could be located. If a numbered ruling is required for audit defense, a targeted follow-up is warranted.
- **AG 2025 reajuste fiscal** = 5,81% per Decreto 0449 de 2026; verify the exact decree number/date against the official Diario Oficial when finalizing, as it was issued close to the report date. It affects activos fijos/ganancias ocasionales, not the ordinary asalariado depuration.
- **AG 2025 filing-window end date** = 26-oct-2026 per the business-day count under Decreto 2229/2023 (vs. the 24-oct end-date of the prior two seasons); confirm against the definitive DIAN 2026 calendar table before hard-coding.
- **Peso conversions** of UVT limits shown here are rounded to the nearest thousand; small differences (±$1.000–$2.000) versus other publishers stem from rounding conventions under art. 868 E.T. — always recompute from the raw `UVT × factor` product in the engine.
- The **Form 210 casilla references** (esp. the internal 43–90 sub-cédula ranges) are drawn from reputable liquidators (Gerencie, Actualícese) cross-referenced to the DIAN instructivo; verify the exact AG 2025 instructivo edition before production use.
